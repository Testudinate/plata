# Appendix I — Current-State Maturity Assessment

## Framework

Plata's data governance maturity is assessed against the DAMA DMBoK framework. Across the 11 DMBoK disciplines, six are tracked actively:

- Change Management
- Data Catalog Management
- Reference Data Management
- Data Quality Management
- Data Architecture
- Master Data Management for Customer Data

For this role, the four dimensions below matter most:

1. **Data Quality Management**
2. **Reference Data Management**
3. **Data Catalog Management**
4. **Change Management**

Each is evaluated on a four-level ladder: **Basic → Defined → Controlled → Continuing improvement**. We assume familiarity with what these levels mean for each discipline; you may use the DMBoK definitions or your own working interpretation, as long as you state which.

## How to use this appendix

For each of the four dimensions:

1. Place Plata at one of the four levels based on the evidence in Appendices A–H.
2. Cite the specific evidence (or its absence) that drove your placement.
3. Where evidence is thin or ambiguous, say so explicitly and describe what you'd want to gather to confirm.
